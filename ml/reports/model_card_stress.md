# Stress model card (stress-v1)

Data: Berka/PKDD’99. Algorithm: XGBoost. Target: next-four-week net digital inflow below 0.35 × M; M is median prior non-overlapping four-week inflow block. Train positive rate: 85.688%. Account-grouped 70/15/15 split, seed 42. This is a derived proxy, not observed default, and Berka is not Indian MSME/UPI validation.

The checked-in saved estimators were evaluated on the original grouped holdout without retraining; see `metrics_saved_model_holdout.json`. That report records SHA-256 hashes for the notebook, saved estimator artifact, and source metrics report used. B-inflow reproduces the source confusion matrix, with ROC-AUC 0.674, average precision 0.915, 50.00% balanced accuracy, and 0% specificity. B-full yields ROC-AUC 0.709, average precision 0.922, 63.08% balanced accuracy, and 59.39% specificity, but its confusion matrix does not match `metrics_stress.json`. The hashes identify the evaluated inputs; they do not establish that the source metrics report was produced from that estimator artifact. Keep the B-full reports distinct until one training run regenerates both together or their separate run provenance is established. Loading the saved artifact emitted XGBoost's warning that it was serialized by an older version; evaluation completed, but re-exporting under a pinned compatible runtime would improve reproducibility. Derived class-balance diagnostics are in `metrics_diagnostics.json`. Raw XGBoost scores are uncalibrated and must not determine policy; capacity math remains deterministic.
