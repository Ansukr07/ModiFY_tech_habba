# ZeroCIBIL

Assumptions inferred from `spec.md` and the F2 booklet. Not organiser copy.

**Product:** Two-sided underwriting desk for credit-invisible MSMEs in India. Owners apply with no CIBIL. Lenders see **which / how much / terms** from verified operations, not PDFs.

**Users:** Kirana / MSME owners; NBFC credit officers. Scene: bright laptop in a shop or a lender open-plan office — **light UI**.

**Mechanism:** Authenticity rails first, lean-month capacity second, terms (limit, tenure, rate, cadence) last. Baseline is reject-if-no-CIBIL.

**Non-goals:** Real AA/GST login, disbursal, blockchain, consumer credit, LLM as underwriter.

**Demo data:** Synthetic, labelled on every screen.
